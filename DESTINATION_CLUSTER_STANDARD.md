# DESTINATION_CLUSTER_STANDARD.md

## Purpose

Every major destination on RejsTilVietnam.dk must be a **content cluster**, not a single introductory article.

Use Nha Trang and Phu Quoc as reference implementations.

Before creating or expanding a destination, also read:
- `STYLE_GUIDE.md`
- `IMAGE_STANDARD.md`
- `EDITORIAL_VISUAL_STANDARD.md`

---

## 1. Required destination architecture

A destination pillar should normally link to dedicated subguides for the questions travellers actually search for.

### Core subguides

For a normal city / region:
1. `sevaerdigheder/` — what to see and what to skip
2. `hvor-skal-man-bo/` — concrete areas, trade-offs and map
3. `bedste-hoteller/` — a small hotel shortlist by area / traveller type
4. `3-dage-i-<destination>/` or `2-dage-i-<destination>/` — realistic itinerary
5. `vejr-og-bedste-rejsetid/` — month/season decisions specific to the destination
6. transport guide — airport / Hanoi transfer / onward travel depending on destination

Add topic-specific pages when the destination deserves them:
- food
- beaches
- trekking
- cruises
- islands/snorkelling
- day trips
- coffee
- history

Do not create a thin page only to hit a URL quota.

---

## 2. Pillar-page standard

The pillar page must be useful on its own and should normally contain:

- short answer / quick facts
- where to stay
- top experiences
- food
- transport
- weather / best travel time
- how many days
- one realistic itinerary or planning recommendation
- contextual images
- video when useful
- links into the destination cluster

A pillar page must not be only a general introduction followed by “read more”.

---

## 3. Subguide standard

Each subguide must answer a distinct user decision.

### Seværdigheder
- 4–8 named places
- prioritisation and trade-offs
- suggested grouping by geography/time
- map
- photos for major places

### Hvor skal man bo
- 3–5 real areas
- who each area suits
- drawbacks
- map
- link to hotel shortlist

### Bedste hoteller
- 4–6 concrete hotels used as reference points
- explain **why** each hotel is included
- distinguish city/base/resort use cases
- map
- do not rank hotels with unsupported “#1 best” claims
- remind readers to check current price/reviews for their dates

### Itinerary
- realistic pacing
- travel time considered
- optional substitutions
- not a giant checklist

### Weather
- destination-specific seasonality
- explain implications for activities
- do not copy a national weather paragraph

### Transport
- concrete origin/destination choices
- practical trade-offs
- airport/station/transfer context
- onward connections where useful

---

## 4. Internal linking

Every destination pillar must link to its subguides.

Every subguide should link back to:
- the destination pillar
- 2–4 adjacent subguides

The site should form a clear topical cluster rather than a collection of orphan articles.

---

## 5. Visual standard

Destination clusters follow `EDITORIAL_VISUAL_STANDARD.md`.

Minimum expectations:
- hero visual
- contextual visuals every 1–2 major sections
- maps when geography matters
- video for atmospheric / movement-heavy topics
- no generic decorative photos when a specific place can be shown

---

## 6. Content specificity

Avoid generic text that could be copied to another city.

Bad:
> “There are many hotels and restaurants in the area.”

Good:
> “Around Tran Phu in Nha Trang you can walk to the city beach and restaurants; the Cam Ranh resort corridor is quieter but is not a practical base for going into Nha Trang every evening.”

Every destination page should contain enough named places, concrete choices and trade-offs that the destination is unmistakable.

---

## 7. QA

A destination cluster is not complete until:
- all routes build
- internal links resolve
- visual-density QA passes
- mobile/tablet/desktop QA passes
- no placeholder destination cards remain
- the pillar exposes the new subguides
- `/alle-guider/` exposes the cluster

Do not merge a new destination as a one-page placeholder if it is intended to be a primary destination on the site.
